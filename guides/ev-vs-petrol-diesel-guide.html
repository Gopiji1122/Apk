EV vs Petrol vs Diesel: The Complete Buyer's Guide

Is an electric car really cheaper? Running cost per km is usually far lower, but the higher purchase price needs time to recover. This guide shows the numbers, the real-world limits and a checklist to decide with confidence.

Quick answer: is an EV cheaper than petrol or diesel?

For running cost, almost always yes. With the default inputs on our calculator an EV costs about ₹2.29 per km all-inclusive, against ₹6.99 for diesel and ₹8.82 for petrol. The catch is the higher purchase price: an EV usually costs lakhs more than the petrol or diesel car you are comparing, and your monthly saving has to pay that extra amount back.

  • Higher monthly km means faster recovery.
  • Charging at home is the single biggest saver.
  • Resale value, battery health and your state's tax waiver decide the final result.

Where does a hybrid fit? EV vs hybrid vs petrol vs diesel

A strong hybrid sits between petrol and EV: it needs no plug, but it still burns fuel. With the default inputs a hybrid costs about ₹5.70 per km all-inclusive, against ₹8.82 for petrol and ₹2.29 for an EV. At 1,200 km a month the EV's extra price over that hybrid recovers in about 4 years 4 months.

  • Choose a hybrid if you cannot charge at home or often take long highway trips.
  • Choose an EV if you can charge at home and drive enough km every month.
  • In the calculator you can compare the EV with any mix: diesel, petrol, hybrid or all three.

How many units for a 0–100% charge? 30, 40 and 50 kWh batteries

One kWh equals one unit on your electricity bill. A charger loses some energy as heat, so the meter records more than the battery stores. With a 12% loss, a 40 kWh battery needs about 45 units from the meter.

Home AC charging (3.3 kW / 7.2 kW) vs public DC fast charging

A 3.3 kW home socket is slow but cheap, usually enough for overnight charging. A 7.2 kW wall box halves the time and needs a proper meter load. Public DC fast chargers are quick (often 10–80% in under an hour) but cost roughly two to three times more per unit.

  • Home AC is best for daily use at about ₹6–9 per unit.
  • Use public DC mainly for highway trips.
  • If most of your charging is public, raise the public share in Advanced options before trusting the savings.

Real-world range: summer AC and 90–110 km/h highway driving

Certified range comes from a test cycle. In daily use, heavy AC in summer and constant 90–110 km/h highway speeds consume more energy per km, so your real range is lower. This also raises your cost per km, because the calculator divides the charge cost by real range. Always enter the range you actually expect, not the brochure number.

Battery life: LFP vs NMC and the 8-year warranty

Two chemistries are common. LFP (lithium iron phosphate) packs tolerate frequent full charges and usually offer many charge cycles; NMC packs store more energy for their weight but prefer routine charging in the 20–80% band.

  • Many brands offer an 8-year battery warranty, often with a km limit and a minimum state of health (SoH).
  • Read what happens below that SoH level, and whether it transfers to a second owner.
  • Battery health at resale affects the price you get, so keep charging habits sensible.

Tyre wear: why EV tyres can cost more per km

EVs are heavier and deliver instant torque, so tyres often wear faster. The calculator assumes 35,000 km for EV tyres and 45,000 km for petrol or diesel. Change both in Advanced options to match your car and driving.

Old car exchange and EV loan EMI

Most buyers fund an EV with the old car's value plus a bank loan. Tab 1 turns your exchange value, pending loan, cash down payment, rate and tenure into an EMI and the true complete cost (price plus all interest).

  • Get 2–3 exchange quotes: dealer, used-car platforms and private buyers.
  • Clear any pending loan first, and ask for the closure letter.
  • Banks often fund up to about 85–90% of the on-road price.
  • A longer tenure lowers EMI but raises total interest; compare 3, 5 and 7 years.

GST, RTO and state tax waivers

Electric cars carry 5% GST, while most petrol and diesel cars are at 18% (small cars) or 40% (larger cars and SUVs) after the September 2025 GST changes. There is no central cash subsidy for private electric cars, so the real benefit usually comes from your state's road tax and registration waiver.

  • State rules change often. As per 2026 reports, Delhi, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra offer waivers, while Karnataka rolled back its full exemption.
  • Some waivers have end dates, so confirm the policy date against your delivery date.
  • Ask the dealer to show road tax and registration fee as zero on the quotation.

Who should wait? A final checklist

  • I have a home or office charging point, or reliable public charging near me.
  • My real monthly km is at least about 1,000.
  • I compared the EV on-road quote with the diesel and petrol quotes.
  • I checked battery warranty (years, km cap, SoH) and my state's tax waiver.
  • My total EMI stays comfortable with my income after insurance and charging.

If you cannot tick most of these, a petrol or diesel car, or a hybrid, may suit you better for now.

Frequently asked questions

How is EV cost per km calculated?

Cost per km = electricity per km (battery units ÷ (1 − charging loss) × price ÷ real range) plus service per km plus tyre cost per km. The calculator shows electricity-only and all-inclusive values.

How many units does a full charge use?

A 30, 40 or 50 kWh battery needs about 34, 45 or 57 units from the meter after a 12% charging loss. At ₹8 per unit that is about ₹273, ₹364 and ₹455.

When will my EV price be recovered?

Divide the extra purchase price by monthly running-cost savings. At 1,200 km a month with the default inputs, the extra price over a diesel car recovers in about 4 years 8 months, but the full EV price takes far longer.

Is the EV better if I drive less than 1,000 km a month?

Savings shrink at low usage, so recovery takes many more years. Check the break-even table in the calculator before deciding.

Can I use my old car as down payment?

Yes. The exchange value minus any pending loan acts as down payment. If the pending loan is higher than the value, the gap is added to the new loan.

Are these results guaranteed?

No. They are estimates. Real savings depend on actual range, driving speed, AC use, battery health, local electricity slabs and final bank loan terms.

Which is better for resale, EV or diesel?

EV resale is still uncertain because battery health matters to used buyers. Use conservative resale percentages in Advanced options and compare the 5-year net cost, not just the running cost.

Can I compare just EV vs diesel, or add a hybrid?

Yes. In Tab 2 the EV is always included. Tick Diesel, Petrol and/or Hybrid to choose which cars to compare. You can compare the EV with one car, two cars or all three.
