    How we calculated (mathematical formulas)

    1. Net EV loan & reducing-balance EMI

    Net Loan (P) = On-Road Price − (Old Car Value − Pending Loan) − Cash Down
    r = Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100, n = Years × 12
    EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1)
    True Complete EV Cost = On-Road Price + (EMI × n − P)

    Example: ₹15,00,000 − ₹3,50,000 − ₹1,50,000 = net loan ₹10,00,000. At 8.90% for 60 months, EMI = ₹20,710; interest = ₹2,42,591; true cost = ₹17,42,591.

    2. EV full charge cost & true cost per km

    Units from meter = Battery kWh ÷ (1 − Loss%)
    Full Charge Cost = Units from meter × Price per unit
    EV Energy/km = Full Charge Cost ÷ Real Range
    EV Cost/km = Energy/km + Annual Service ÷ (Monthly km × 12) + Tyre Set Cost ÷ Tyre Life

    Example: 40 ÷ (1 − 0.12) = 45.5 units × ₹8 = ₹364; ÷ 300 km = ₹1.21/km. Add service ₹0.28/km and tyres ₹0.80/km = ₹2.29/km.

    3. Diesel & petrol all-inclusive cost per km

    Fuel/km = Fuel Price ÷ Mileage
    Cost/km = Fuel/km + Annual Service ÷ (Monthly km × 12) + Tyre Set Cost ÷ Tyre Life
    Hybrid: same formula with the hybrid's own mileage and fuel price

    Example (diesel): 90 ÷ 16 = ₹5.63/km fuel + service + tyres = ₹6.99/km. Petrol: 100 ÷ 13 = ₹7.69/km fuel + service + tyres = ₹8.82/km.

    4. Extra price recovery & 100% complete EV price recovery

    Monthly Saving = (ICE Cost/km − EV Cost/km) × Monthly km
    Extra Price = (EV Price + Charger) − ICE Car Price
    Extra Price Recovery (months) = Extra Price ÷ Monthly Saving
    100% Complete EV Price Recovery (months) = (EV Price + Charger) ÷ Monthly Saving
    Recovery km = Months × Monthly km

    Example vs diesel: monthly saving ₹5,642; extra price ₹3,15,000 ÷ ₹5,642 = 55.8 months; full EV price ₹15,15,000 ÷ ₹5,642 = 268.5 months.

    EV switching factors: what changes real savings
    • Real range, not claimed range. Summer AC and 90–110 km/h highway driving can cut range noticeably compared with a gentle city run. Enter your real-world range, not the brochure figure.
    • Where you will charge. Home charging at ₹6–9/unit is the biggest saver. Public DC fast charging costs two to three times more per unit. If you cannot charge at home, add a public charging share in Advanced options.
    • Battery type and warranty. LFP packs usually last more charge cycles and tolerate 100% charging; NMC packs pack more range but prefer 20–80% daily use. Many brands give an 8-year battery warranty with a km cap and a minimum health (SoH) level, so read the terms.
    • Tyres wear faster. EVs are heavier with instant torque. The calculator assumes 35,000 km tyre life for EV and 45,000 km for petrol/diesel.
    • Taxes and RTO. EVs carry 5% GST against 18% or 40% on petrol/diesel cars. Road tax and registration waivers differ by state and change often, so confirm what your dealer quote actually includes.
    • Resale and your usage. Higher monthly km shortens recovery time. Below roughly 800–1,000 km a month, the extra price may take many years to recover.

    Quick buying checklist

    • I have a home or office charging point, or reliable public charging near me.
    • My real monthly km is at least about 1,000.
    • I compared the EV on-road quote with the diesel and petrol quotes.
    • I checked battery warranty (years, km cap, SoH) and my state's tax waiver.
    • My total EMI stays comfortable with my income after insurance and charging.
    Frequently asked questions

    How is EV cost per km calculated?

    Cost per km = electricity per km (battery units ÷ (1 − charging loss) × price ÷ real range) plus service per km plus tyre cost per km. The calculator shows electricity-only and all-inclusive values.

    How many units does a full charge use?

    A 30, 40 or 50 kWh battery needs about 34, 45 or 57 units from the meter after a 12% charging loss. At ₹8 per unit that is about ₹273, ₹364 and ₹455.

    When will my EV price be recovered?

    Divide the extra purchase price by monthly running-cost savings. At 1,200 km a month with the default inputs, the extra price over a diesel car recovers in about 4 years 8 months, but the full EV price takes far longer.

    Is the EV better if I drive less than 1,000 km a month?

    Savings shrink at low usage, so recovery takes many more years. Check the break-even table in the calculator before deciding.

    Can I use my old car as down payment?

    Yes. The exchange value minus any pending loan acts as down payment. If the pending loan is higher than the value, the gap is added to the new loan.

    Are these results guaranteed?

    No. They are estimates. Real savings depend on actual range, driving speed, AC use, battery health, local electricity slabs and final bank loan terms.

    Can I compare just EV vs diesel, or add a hybrid?

    Yes. In Tab 2 the EV is always included. Tick Diesel, Petrol and/or Hybrid to choose which cars to compare. You can compare the EV with one car, two cars or all three.
